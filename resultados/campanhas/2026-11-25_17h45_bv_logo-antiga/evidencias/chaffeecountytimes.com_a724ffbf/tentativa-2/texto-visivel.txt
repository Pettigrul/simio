BVHS cross country competes at regionals, Nudge qualifies for state
Jack Sellner, Sports Desk Oct 26, 2026 Updated Oct 26, 2026  0
Facebook
Twitter
Email
Facebook
Twitter
Email
Print
Copy article link
Save

State qualifier Leo Nudge led the way for the Buena Vista High School cross-country team at the Colorado 2A Region 5 meet Oct. 23.

The Demons made the two and a half hour drive to Delta for their regional meet with a chance to send athletes to the state meet the following weekend. Though neither the boys or the girls’ teams qualified, Nudge, a senior, finished top 15 individually and secured his spot at the Oct. 31 state meet. 

“The meet went well, especially given that we were in a larger “region,” running against more runners, in a town that's nearly on the other side of the state,” wrote head coach Mallory Brooks in an email to the Times.

Nudge led the way for the boys, finishing seventh overall with a time of 18 minutes and 24.97 seconds. Ben Moore finished next, placing 32nd with a time of 19:46.05. Nathan Whiten placed 41st with a time of 20:16.09 and Simon Allen placed 81st with a time of 23:11.14.

The boys finished 10th out of the 13 teams that scored at the meet.

Brooks emphasized that she had a very specific plan for Nudge in the race that the two of them had been perfecting throughout the year and he executed it in Delta.

“In Leo's case, his goal was to start slow and build,” wrote Brooks. “Finish mile 1 around 30th place, then work his way up to 20th by the end of mile 2. Then just start cranking and passing everyone he can for the last 1.1 miles. He did exactly that, which led to a solid, very impressive finish and a secured spot” for the state meet.

On the girls side, Maya Lyle led the way with a 29th place finish and a time of 24:21.73. Right behind her was Emerson Fritch who placed 30th with a time of 24:41.62, then Lilly Lazaruis placed 42nd with a time of 27:08.91 and Madi Lazaruis placed 44th with a time of 27:14.02. 

Lilly Lazaruis rescheduled a doctor's appointment to race on Friday and Brooks emphasized that she was fully prepared in the case that all four of her runners didn’t cross the finish line.

“Not only did she finish, but she ran her fastest race neck and neck with her sister,” said Brooks of Lilly Lazaruis. “Sometimes, when you clear away all of the expectations and pressures, you perform at your highest.”

With the exception of Nudge, the 2026 cross country season is now over for the Demons. Seniors Lyle, Lilly Lazaruis and Brantley Gaston raced for the final time this year and left an impact on the team.

“For Lilly, Maya and Brantley, their cross country season is over, unfortunately,” said Brooks. “I have felt like the luckiest coach in the world, having had them on my team.”

Nudge will complete his season and represent Buena Vista for the final time in 2026 in Colorado Springs Oct. 31, at the Colorado High School Activities Association State Championships.
