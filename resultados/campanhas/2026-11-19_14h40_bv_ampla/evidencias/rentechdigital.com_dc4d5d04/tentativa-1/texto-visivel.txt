List of Customs brokers in Paraná
There are 824 Customs brokers in Paraná as of April 1, 2026; which is an 3.39% increase from 2023. Of these locations, 754 Customs brokers which is 91.50% of all Customs brokers in Paraná are single-owner operations, while the remaining 70 which is 8.50% are part of larger brands. The top three cities with the most Customs brokers are Curitiba with 337 Customs brokers, Londrina with 69 Customs brokers, Cascavel with 37 Customs brokers. Average age of Customs brokers in Paraná is 4 years and 5 months.
Customs brokers are also establishing a strong digital presence across various platforms: 24 have LinkedIn profiles, 109 have Facebook Pages, 131 are active on Instagram, 26 have X (formerly Twitter) handles, 2 are on TikTok, and 34 have YouTube channels. About 351 Customs brokers have their own website and the remaining 473 don’t have their own website.
