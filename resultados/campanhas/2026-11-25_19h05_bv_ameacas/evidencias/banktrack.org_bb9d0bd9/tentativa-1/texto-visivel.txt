Standard Bank
South Africa

In October, NGOs filed a formal complaint with South Africa’s Advertising Regulatory Board (ARB) challenging Standard Bank over misleading environmental and climate claims which contrast with its continued financing of major new fossil fuel projects such as the EACOP.
