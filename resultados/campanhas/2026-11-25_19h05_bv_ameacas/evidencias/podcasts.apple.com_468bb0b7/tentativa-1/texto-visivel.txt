What’s Behind All the Tech Outages?

More than 417,000 US flights are scheduled during the Thanksgiving holiday period this year, according to the Transportation Department. But the system US air traffic controllers use to track those flights has only recently started switching to digital strips and a third of communications still run over decades-old copper wiring. On today’s Big Take, Sarah Holder talks with Bloomberg aviation policy reporter Allyson Versprille about how the US air traffic control system got so out of date. Cyber editor Jeff Stone also joins to discuss why the race to replace aging technology — in government, as well as the private sector — is fueling a rise in tech outages.
