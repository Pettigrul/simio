$1,000 Visit Grant

Just for the Class of 2027. High school seniors who join us for a campus tour, open house, or other official Admissions event are eligible for a $1,000 visit grant toward your 2027-2028 tuition. There's never been a better time to visit!

(Eligibility: The $1,000 Visit Grant is available exclusively to high school seniors in the graduating Class of 2027 who complete an official daily campus visit or attend an official Admissions event day between May 1, 2026, and May 1, 2027. Visits must be scheduled through the Office of Admissions to qualify. The grant is applied toward tuition for the 2027–2028 academic year only.)
