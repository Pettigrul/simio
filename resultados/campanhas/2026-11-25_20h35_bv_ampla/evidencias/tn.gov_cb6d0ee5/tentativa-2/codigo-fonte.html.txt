    <div class="tn-rte">
    
        <h2>What is the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)?&nbsp;</h2>
<p>Formerly referred to as “food stamps,” the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) is a U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) nutritional assistance initiative administered at the state level. In Tennessee, SNAP is managed by the Tennessee Department of Human Services (TDHS).</p>
<p>At TDHS, we administer SNAP with the belief that you can focus on work, school, and family wellness when you’re well-nourished and don’t have to worry about where your next meal will come from. Excelling at your job, training or higher education can increase career prospects, earning potential, and levels of self-sufficiency. These opportunities can help your family transition from today’s challenges into a brighter future tomorrow.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Use the menu and information on this page to learn more about SNAP <a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">application</a>, <a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap-eligibility-information.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">eligibility</a><span style="">, </span><a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/snap-electronic-benefit-transfer-ebt-cards.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">EBT cards</a><span style="">, </span><a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/tdhs-disaster-emergency-assistance.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">emergency programing</a>, and other related topics.</p>

    
    

</div>


    
    
    <div class="tn-horizontalrulev2 separator">

    <div class="tn-rte">
    
        <h2>Who Can Receive SNAP</h2>
<p>SNAP is available to households (individuals who live and prepare food together) that meet certain qualifications referred to as eligibility criteria. SNAP eligibility takes residency, age and relationship, citizenship, social security number assignment, employment, resource, income, and related deductions factors into consideration. These factors not only determine a household’s eligibility to receive SNAP but also determine if additional documents will be required for a household to maintain SNAP after SNAP approval.</p>
<p><b>Failure to submit required documents such as <a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/information-and-resources/dhs-forms-and-applications.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">change forms</a> or Simplified Reporting Forms can result in the cancellation of SNAP benefits.</b></p>
<p>Visit our&nbsp;<a title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap-eligibility-information.html" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">Eligibility Information</a> web page to learn more about SNAP eligibility.</p>

    
    

</div>


    
    
    <div class="tn-horizontalrulev2 separator">

    <hr class="cmp-separator__horizontal-rule">

    <div class="tn-rte">
    
        <h2>Applying for SNAP</h2>
<p>You can apply for SNAP by&nbsp;completing and submitting a SNAP application online&nbsp;via the <a title="Continue to external site in new tab" href="http://onedhs.tn.gov" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">One DHS Customer Portal</a>&nbsp;<strong>OR</strong>&nbsp;you may submit a printed application by uploading or mailing a <a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/information-and-resources/dhs-forms-and-applications.html" target="_self" title="Continue to internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">printed application to TDHS.</a> <strong>To receive a printed application, you may&nbsp;</strong><a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/office-locator-family-assistance.html" target="_self" title="Continue to internal site in the same tab" aria-label="Continue to internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>contact</strong></a><strong>&nbsp;your local office to have an application mailed to you or visit the <a title="Continue to internal site in the same tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">Applying for Services</a> page to download a SNAP application in the language of your choosing.</strong></p>
<p>TDHS also works with local organizations, agencies, and advocates across Tennessee to share information about SNAP and provide application support. The State's Outreach partners can provide pre-screening and application assistance for individuals and families. <strong><a title="Open PDF in new tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/human-services/documents/SNAP%20Outreach%20Partners%203.11.2025.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Find a SNAP Outreach partner near you.</a></strong></p>
<p><strong>After you submit your SNAP application, other documentation and actions may be required to process your application and maintain your benefits. Visit our <a title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">Applying for Services web page</a> to&nbsp;learn more about the SNAP application process and related form and documentation requirements.</strong></p>

        <h2>Preparing to Apply for SNAP</h2>
<p>To receive SNAP benefits, applicants are required to submit copies of personal records (verification documents) that demonstrate a need for SNAP. These eligibility verification documents should include&nbsp;<b>copies</b> of record/document that show proof of the following:</p>
<ul><li><b>Social Security Number&nbsp;</b>(Example: Social Security Card)</li><li><b>Your Identification&nbsp;</b>(Example: driver’s license, school ID with&nbsp;photo, passport, resident alien card, I-94 card, voter’s registration card)</li><li><b>Where you live&nbsp;</b>(Example: lease agreement, utility bill, phone bill, or driver’s license with your address on it) &nbsp;</li><li><b>Income&nbsp;</b>(Example: check stubs, employer statement, award letters)</li><li><b>Resources&nbsp;</b>(Example: bank statements, title, registration, or deed)</li><li><b>Shelter costs&nbsp;</b>(Example:&nbsp;mortgage payments, property tax, homeowner’s insurance, rent receipt, lease agreement)</li><li><b>Cost of utilities&nbsp;</b>(Example: electric bill, water bill, gas bill)</li></ul>
<p>It’s best to have copies of these documents ready for submission when you apply for SNAP. Submitting verification documents within 10 days of applying for SNAP can help the application process go smoothly and may prevent delays. You may also be asked to provide more information during an eligibility interview. View the&nbsp;<a title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap-eligibility-information.html" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">SNAP eligibility</a>&nbsp;webpage for more information.&nbsp; &nbsp;</p>
<p>Uploading documents using the&nbsp;<a href="https://onedhs.tn.gov/csp" target="_blank" title="Continue to external site in new tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">One DHS Customer Portal</a>&nbsp;or resources at a&nbsp;<a href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/need-help-/tdhs-find-our-offices.html" target="_self" title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" aria-label="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" rel="noopener noreferrer">TDHS office</a>&nbsp;can help save time. Customers who choose to mail information to TDHS as part of the application, verification or reporting process should send <b>copies </b>of personal records and avoid sending original documents as documents may not be returned.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

        <h2>Preventing, Reporting and Stopping EBT Theft and Fraud</h2>
<p>The Tennessee Department of Human Services (TDHS) is here to ensure Tennesseans have the tools they need to become financially stable and uses Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT) cards to deliver benefits. Unfortunately, some criminals are willing to use card skimming and other tactics to steal aid meant to help families on their way to financial stability. Please Note: TDHS will not call or text message customers to request their EBT card number or pin. If you are contacted by someone by phone, text or email requesting your EBT card information, it could be a scam.</p>
<p>Customers and businesses that use and accept benefits using EBT cards are strongly encouraged to review preventative measures and reporting practices to stop the theft of benefits. Visit our <a title="Continue to Internal site in the same tab" href="https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/need-help-/tdhs-fraud.html" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">Do You Suspect Fraud web page</a> to learn how to prevent, report and stop EBT theft and fraud.</p>

        <h2>SNAP Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents (ABAWD)&nbsp;Work Requirements</h2>
<p><b>Due to implementation of HR1, there are changes for ABAWD eligibility. Your benefits could be impacted if you do not upload verification documents </b>via the <b><a title="Continue to external site in new tab" href="https://onedhs.tn.gov/csp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">One DHS Customer Portal</a></b> no later than <b>October 31, 2025</b>.</p>
<ul><li><b>An Important Note for Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents (ABAWD):&nbsp;</b>The ABAWD time clock resets effective January 1,&nbsp;2025.&nbsp;An individual who is &nbsp;ABAWD is an able-bodied person between the ages of 18 and 64 without children under the age of 14 in the household. You or any household member between the ages of 18 through 64 may be considered an Able-Bodied Adult Without Dependents (ABAWD). An ABAWD can only be eligible for three (3) months of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP, formerly known as food stamp) benefits in a three (3) year period unless they are meeting the SNAP work requirement. Many SNAP ABAWDS are already complying with the work requirement. The ABAWD SNAP work requirement is: Work or volunteer with a reputable source at least 20 hours a week (80 hours per month) or more,</li><li>Participate in a qualified training program for an average of 20 hours per week (80 hours per month) or more, OR</li><li>Participate in a combination of work, volunteer, and/or training program for an average of 20 hours per week (80 hours per month).</li></ul>
<p>In Tennessee, the new time limit for ABAWD (able-bodied adults without dependents) will begin January 1, 2025 to December 31, 2027. &nbsp;ABAWD customers may lose SNAP benefits after three months if they do not adhere to the updated ABAWD work requirements.</p>
