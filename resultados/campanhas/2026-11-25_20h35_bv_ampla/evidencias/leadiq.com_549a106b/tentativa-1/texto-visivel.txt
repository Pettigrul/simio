Banco BV is a sizable financial institution based in São Paulo, Brazil, with more than 37 years of experience in Latin American banking. It offers a broad suite of financial products, including credit for vehicles and solar energy, as well as accounts, cards, loans, financing, insurance, and corporate and investment banking. The company emphasizes agility, digital capabilities, and a client-centered approach, seeking to convert credit into enduring relationships through a balance of technology, people, and responsibility. Its client base comprises individuals and businesses seeking financing to realize personal goals and pursue new ventures, with a focus on lasting partnerships. Headquartered in São Paulo, the bank has been recognized as the Best Institution to Work for by GPTW in 2024 and 2025, and in August 2022 Bradesco announced a joint asset-management venture with Banco BV.
