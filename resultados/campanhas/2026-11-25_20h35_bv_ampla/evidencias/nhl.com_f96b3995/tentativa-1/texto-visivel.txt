Suzuki has three points in return, Canadiens rally for OT win against Mammoth

Caufield scores at 30 seconds after Engstrom ties it with 5:06 left in third; Cooley gets hat trick for Utah
Mammoth at Canadiens | Recap
By
Sean Farrell
/ NHL.com Independent Correspondent
November 6, 2026

MONTREAL -- Nick Suzuki had a goal and two assists in his return from an upper-body injury for the Montreal Canadiens, who rallied late despite Logan Cooley’s hat trick for a 5-4 overtime win against the Utah Mammoth at Bell Centre on Thursday.
