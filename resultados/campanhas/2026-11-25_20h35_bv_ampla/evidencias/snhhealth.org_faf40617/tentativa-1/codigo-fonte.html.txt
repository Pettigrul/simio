    <h2 class="h2">Committed. Connected. Patient-Focused Care.</h2>

<p>Southern New Hampshire Health provides comprehensive, high quality, high value healthcare to communities in southern New Hampshire and northern Massachusetts. As a non-profit health system that has provided care for more than 130 years, we are dedicated to providing access to a wide range of care options for all.</p>

<p>Southern New Hampshire Medical Center,&nbsp;a 188-bed acute care facility located in the heart of downtown Nashua, has a medical staff of over 500 primary and specialty care providers from Foundation Medical Partners, Dartmouth-Hitchcock Nashua, and local independent practices.</p>

<p>Foundation Medical Partners&nbsp;is a multi-specialty provider group, including more than 300 providers in primary, specialty and immediate care serving thousands of patients in more than 70 practices across southern New Hampshire and northern Massachusetts.</p>

<p>To deliver on our mission, we continue to innovate and support our skilled providers who bring the very best to our communities to help patients remain closer to home for their care.</p>
