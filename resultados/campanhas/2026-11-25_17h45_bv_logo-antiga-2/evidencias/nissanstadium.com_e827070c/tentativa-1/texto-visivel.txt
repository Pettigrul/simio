SHUTTLES

GRAY LINE TENNESSEE GAMEDAY SHUTTLE

Enjoy a convenient and stress-free ride to Nissan Stadium with the Gray Line Tennessee Gameday Shuttle
opens in a new tab
.

Continuous service begins 90 minutes prior to kickoff
Return service begins at halftime through 1 hour post-game
Cost: $20 roundtrip per person (receipt serves as ticket)
Children 6 and under ride free
Cash is not accepted; QR codes will be available on-site for purchase

Please note: Shuttle ticket purchase does not guarantee parking availability.

NEW! PARK & RIDE SHUTTLE WITH GRAY LINE & PMC

Gray Line Tennessee and PMC offer convenient gameday park & ride shuttle options from downtown Nashville and Franklin/Cool Springs to and from Nissan Stadium.

Locations: 1221 Broadway & 1800 Galleria Blvd.
Cost: $50 per vehicle, plus taxes (1221 Broadway) & $70 per vehicle, plus taxes (Franklin/Cool Springs)
Departure Times: 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM
Stadium Drop-Off & Return Boarding: Woodland St. and S. 1st St.
Tickets: Your parking lot receipt serves as your ticket. Please have it ready to show when parking and boarding. Purchasing in advance is encouraged.

Purchase Gameday Park & Ride Tickets here.

WEGO PUBLIC TRANSIT

Public transport through WeGo provides a convenient service to and from Titans games at Nissan Stadium. There are three ways you can roll with WeGo, including FREE local bus service, an Express Train with stops east of downtown from Donelson to Lebanon, and Park & Ride Express Bus from four surrounding areas – Bellevue and Hickory Hollow (Free), Cool Springs and Gallatin/Hendersonville ($2 each way). In total, fans can choose from nine local and express routes connecting directly to Nissan Stadium. Bus drop-off and pick-up is on Woodland Street and S. 1st St., by the bridge, with WeGo staff on-site to help.

Visit WeGoTransit.com/Community/Nissan-Stadium
opens in a new tab
 for all the details!

WATER TRANSPORTATION

Several Cumberland River operators offer boat service to the stadium on Titans gamedays, docking within walking distance of the venue. Schedules and pricing vary by operator.


Search “Cumberland River water transportation” for current providers, availability, schedules, and ticketing. Details may vary by event and operator. Not affiliated with or operated by Nissan Stadium or the Tennessee Titans.

ACCESSIBLE OPTIONS

Accessible Parking

Accessible parking is available in stadium lots with a valid parking pass and is offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Parking passes must be purchased in advance
opens in a new tab
.

Accessible Drop-Off & Pick-Up in Lot T

The ADA drop-off and pick-up is located in Lot T
opens in a new tab
. Directions:

Travel southbound on S. 5th St from Shelby Ave
Turn right onto Davidson St and proceed to Lot T

Please have your stadium-issued ADA Lane Pass ready for law enforcement at roadblocks. To receive a stadium-issued ADA Lane pass, please send us a message requesting one, along with the name of the event you are attending. This Lane Pass does not include on-site parking.

WeGo Access

WeGo Access offers specialized vans for persons with disabilities within Davidson County who are unable to use fixed-route transit services. The cost is $3.70 per ride. Schedule 1 to 7 days in advance by calling 615-880-3970. Visit WeGoTransit.com
opens in a new tab
 to learn more.

For additional stadium accessibility information, please visit our Accessibility page.
