Plaza Hotel & Casino

The Plaza’s 1 Main Street address is literally the birthplace of Las Vegas. Originally the site of the Union Pacific train depot, in 1971 it became home to the Union Plaza, which was the world’s largest hotel-casino at the time. Later renamed to the Plaza Hotel & Casino, today it remains an eye-catching property under the leadership of CEO Jonathan Jossel. Western Alliance Bank has been part of their story since 2010, bringing a responsive, relationship-driven approach that has supported the Plaza’s bold ideas with reliable execution.

“I’ve been banking with Western Alliance since 2010, and with everyone I’ve worked with, the relationship’s always been incredible. No matter what we’ve needed or what part of the business it’s related to, we’ve always had great service.” Jonathan Jossel CEO Plaza Hotel & Casino
