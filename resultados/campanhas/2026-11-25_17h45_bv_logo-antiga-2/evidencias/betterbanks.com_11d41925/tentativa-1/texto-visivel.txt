What You Need to Know About Trump Accounts
Trump Accounts are a new type of individual retirement account for children established by the Working Families Tax Cuts Act of 2025. The accounts are designed to help children begin long-term investing and building financial security early in life, and to learn about financial markets through real-world participation. +
