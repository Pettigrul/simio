About

Banco BV, formerly known as Banco Votorantim, is a Brazilian bank that offers a range of financial services including consumer finance, corporate banking, treasury, and investment banking solutions. Headquartered in São Paulo, the bank serves individuals and companies through credit, financing, and investment products, as well as partnerships with other financial institutions and distribution channels across Brazil.
