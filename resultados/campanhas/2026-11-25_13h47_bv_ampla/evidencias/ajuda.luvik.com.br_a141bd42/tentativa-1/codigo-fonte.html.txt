<section>
<p>A BV financeira possui uma solução chamada&nbsp;<span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;" href="https://meufinanciamentosolar.com.br/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Meu Financiamento Solar</a>.</span> O&nbsp;Meu Financiamento Solar é uma plataforma digital exclusiva, para você financiar o seu sistema de energia solar.</p>
<p>Após criado o cadastro na plataforma da BV, a solução permite realizar a simulação do financiamento de duas formas.</p>
<ol>
<li>Através de um link para acessar o simulador.</li>
<li>Adicionando o simulador dentro do seu site.</li>
</ol>
<div>
<h4 style="font-size: 28px; line-height: 36.4px; color: #232529; font-family: Poppins, sans-serif; font-weight: 600; text-transform: none; font-style: normal;">Através de um link para acessar o simulador</h4>
</div>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Uma vez que você possui o link para fazer a simulação, é possível adicionar este link na sua proposta comercial. Este link será exibido na proposta e ao clicar nele, o cliente poderá fazer a simulação direto no site da BV. É importante ressaltar que o link só é exibido na versão WEB da proposta comercial.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Para adicionar o link na sua proposta, acesse </span><b>“Configurações &gt; Bancos/Financeiras”</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Depois, caso ainda não esteja selecionada, selecione a BV Financeira e clique em “</span><b>Salvar</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">”. Depois de salvo, será habilitado o botão de “</span><b>Configurar</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">”.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border: none; display: inline-block; overflow: hidden; width: 815px; height: 66px;"><img decoding="async" style="margin-left: 0px; margin-top: 0px;" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXePr6KMpDQoON3b8YFYN2HVv3dBqc23JAGuwTNp0cw-Wc0kL4BwJdIEOKTysDI35CHHg4B5UvFBx81k3uBBRlZMlkYKgTUdVudjSZTCsrqZuzRsFf3gf_8Nncopvx-JYxcxm7lU?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="815" height="66"></span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adicione o link fornecido pela BV financeira no campo abaixo e clique em “</span><b>Salvar</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">”.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border: none; display: inline-block; overflow: hidden; width: 656px; height: 335px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="margin-left: 0px; margin-top: 0px;" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcEf3AFwdyhuH7LkHKzuFiqoGSGQxT7ZYd7I3LzVYI3YdhVwIzTNhE1W6nRSEYDWf41FiInlk9sSQq24G96RJN6x4DDpjuR9L9dKuYk3JyFR7By07gNX3wiS7jQvBS1Co4RugfHSA?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" alt="mceclip0.png" width="656" height="335"></span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Para copiar o link de simulação, logue no seu portal da BV, acesse</span><b> “Simulador no seu site &gt; Link da empresa”</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> e depois copie o link.</span></p>
<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-8afd3bfc-7fff-ea45-75b7-7dc1e18b9edf"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:256px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Login_Instalador_-_Meu_Financiamento_Solar.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeXy1_lTGSEch62u3YElBMZrTZkXS3zK6CW0qs9IOKvZb85bIegL4hsY6CPSXBpdzbxicAKlhYaY3hoyO8MTbyQdL6fjwXZOaud9tGsDDa5rGNIikJA6SMRXChpSIbLpennuXpaIg?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="256" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></p>
<p>No campo&nbsp;<strong>“Link do Simulador (URL iFrame)”</strong>&nbsp;adicione o endereço do seu site do Luvik e clique em <b>“Salvar”</b>.</p>
<p>Por exemplo:&nbsp;<span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a style="color: #0000ff;" href="https://meusite.com.br/">https://meusite.com.br/</a>&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><span id="docs-internal-guid-c0afce0a-7fff-663d-b56c-a82dd475bd9b"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; text-decoration-skip-ink: none; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:152px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfhfuooyft-HmR_KTPxJ35HkLzgddPaCNNiwKn46qYM1q696dCsMedFOK8Yt6FRXA3is5Uk1LfwESUG2xVQK1o4l6rBDxVu7aCAFeWKR1PoTft_PC2lLzxHjip-SOYAUq5sT300-w?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="152" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></span></p>

<h4 style="font-size: 28px; line-height: 36.4px; color: #232529; font-family: Poppins, sans-serif; font-weight: 600; text-transform: none; font-style: normal;">Adicionando o simulador dentro do seu site</h4>
</div>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Para os clientes que possuem o </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a style="color: #0000ff;" href="https://ajuda.luvik.com.br/site-luvik/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">site gerado pelo Luvik</span></a></span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, é possível adicionar o simulador da BV dentro do site. Para isto, acesse novamente o portal da BV financeira para copiar o código de iFrame.</span></p>
<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-8de2c221-7fff-9247-0015-f308ad7f1d87"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:237px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Login_Instalador_-_Meu_Financiamento_Solar.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdeJ2w-xGz97iJoArV-3LLHysLI0q3GayCVsTPbOllBPUSuNnGhslvD6JKLexcX3ms5soDPZ3XTRABtjp8WuBq1UfmfMUK2ehrADY-QcvPddSysV-Kftuf7WJ-6IAbFEzKJZW9a?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="237" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></p>
<p>Informe o endereço do seu site e copie o código do iFrame.</p>
<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-b52f2c57-7fff-b36b-181e-4b2f8ee5fd09"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:275px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Login_Instalador_-_Meu_Financiamento_Solar.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcz8wkPAeRrN8C9bu8xSvVyyq07VO9HURYGLjbjiJSCjLbvRzQ6Ms15vXKJaFJdOXA0Bz_RKijtLCWjpIX-zPQLgapqCVxMFHVIawUO6btDGxthZF86bzFq65UYPYh2stDrwN-4Fg?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="275" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dentro do Luvik, acesse “</span><b>Configurações &gt; Meu site</b><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">”</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Cole o código do iFrame no campo “</span><b>iFrame simulador de financiamento BV</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">”.</span></p>
<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-e7991761-7fff-cf35-3d93-a4d5a1560b8a"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:245px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="mceclip0.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcV0Z3IUwlpt4GRFYrh6UVayNgB44S9VpOtcwGGXSS3_dvnaspkxJPGosbgFrPf88-6REK0fWYIf_KWSuAig2S2V0GG5bMXGF73z1EKdZ9eQPHbYzsqNKEN0UpkEHxuN0rAkmNWwQ?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="245" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span><span style="font-weight: 400;"></span></p>
<p><br></p>
<p>Para que o iFrame fique melhor no seu site, recomendamos que seja alterado a sua altura para 1000px. O valor padrão na altura é de 800px, a imagem abaixo mostra a alteração que pode ser feita.</p>
<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-ace5b8e4-7fff-e00f-d316-9714f0b4158c"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:247px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="mceclip2.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXexNxy19C02JkJUteeo0pHu4-E5oJ8cXKCa4k3PptxxmUm2bxcV4R35_2iv7zFUQit2YZZGrctmIlwNxBWU0D0bC3lcdhHRXt_GnNqH1QPJ75NAqFQ5vyMBlsDRyXxTiZkP4WCtHw?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="247" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></p>

<p><span id="docs-internal-guid-e4b0ebdf-7fff-f679-6768-e5da7959f00e"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif; background-color: transparent; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space-collapse: preserve;"><span style="border:none;display:inline-block;overflow:hidden;width:602px;height:499px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="mceclip0.png" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXe1EltCMxRGoArVas_Q96mib6TXknSrJPPLFQJNdGCg92DUhQ1kmcfAFl7R9p0mlx4KfCQ3OJB19aZT1Mf_1teaEXKjRiri6j44WcAZFsQWV2v3bir2x4-zhkpZtTDdfU-CPRIALw?key=YoUN8dCr6xa2a6REyxSYww" width="602" height="499" style="margin-left:0px;margin-top:0px;"></span></span></span></p>
