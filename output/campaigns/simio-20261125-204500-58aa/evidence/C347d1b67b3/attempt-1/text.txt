Hutson's second of game lifts Canadiens to OT victory against Hurricanes

Defenseman wins it at 38 seconds, Dobes makes 26 saves for Montreal
Canadiens at Hurricanes | Recap
By

/ NHL.com Independent Correspondent
November 19, 2026

RALEIGH, N.C. -- Lane Hutson scored his second goal of the game 38 seconds into overtime, and the Montreal Canadiens defeated the Carolina Hurricanes 2-1 at Lenovo Center on Thursday.
